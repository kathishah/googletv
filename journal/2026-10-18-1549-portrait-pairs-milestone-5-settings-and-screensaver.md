Built and installed as before (199 JVM tests pass; lint 0 errors, 26 warnings, unchanged). The settings screen gained a **Layout** row (Single, Portrait pairs) between Interval and Label, saved with the rest on **Save** and discarded by **Cancel**; the DreamService builds the same `PairingConfig` when the preference is Portrait pairs, so the layout applies to the screensaver (its photos are photos only; with Type other than Photos it applies to the 500 random photos). - **Screen fit (screenshots):** seven rows, the note beside the Layout buttons, and the Preview, Save, Cancel row all fit. - **Idle activation with pairs (60000 ms timeout, original 600000 noted first):** `KEYCODE_HOME` at 15:41:37, `dumpsys power` read `Dreaming` with `SlideshowDreamService` by 15:42:38 (the first poll after the minute; polls send no input). The dream logged `Layout Portrait pairs: 808 slides from 942 (134 pairs, 435 portrait photos)`; in 4.5 minutes it showed 3 pairs and 17 singles, every one 10.0 s after the previous (the Interval) except the pairs that needed two decodes: 11.0 s, 19.4 s (two 24 MP HEICs, decode 9.0 + 10.2 s), and one single at 15.7 s after a slow decode. Three pair screenshots were viewed: lattice, oak frame, pinstripes, upright, labels inside their photos. No crash.
- **Single unchanged in the screensaver:** with the user's original settings (no `layout_mode`), the dream started at the 60 s mark, read the albums (`Album slides ready: 942 photos`), showed a landscape photo with its label, and logged no layout, pair, or slide line at all (those are logged only with pairing on).
- **Restored:** `slideshow.xml` and the real v5 catalog compared identical (`cmp`) to the copies taken before milestone 1, the timeout is 600000 ms again, and the DreamService is still selected. (Layout is on Single; the final build keeps it there.)
- **Not covered:** the 10-minute default activation with pairs (the 60 s timeout was used, as in earlier idle checks), a screensaver run with Type Photos + Videos (500 random photos; same player code), and an idle run of hours.
