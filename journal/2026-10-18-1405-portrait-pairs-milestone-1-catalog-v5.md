The saved v4 catalog was rejected by the new build, and opening Choose Albums rebuilt it automatically (the same flow as the picker's Refresh).

- **Query check on the Mac, in place:** the portrait query run read-only against the clone's `Photos.sqlite` (`mode=ro&immutable=1`, the last checkpoint, no copy) returned 9,106 rows before exclusions. A first version of the SQL filtered on shorter side over longer side, which keeps 4:3 landscape photos too (25,248 rows); the shipped query applies the orientation swap in SQL.
- **Refresh on the TV:** total 63.8 s (database and WAL reused, 0 bytes fetched). SQLite's first read (WAL index) 40.0 s, album rows 3.2 s, videos 1.9 s, exclusion 4.5 s, **portrait query 10.4 s** (9,127 rows read), album mapping 0.4 s, catalog write 0.5 s. The 759 albums, 2,667 library videos, 3,862 album video memberships, and 2,459 excluded paths are unchanged.

- **24-path sample against the real files:** one to two paths per folder, shuffled, 24 in all (the paths stayed in scratch). Their raw pixel sizes from the files (`sips`) matched the database's `ZORIGINALWIDTH`/`ZORIGINALHEIGHT` in **24 of 24**. The database orientations were 6 (19 files), 8 (4), and 1 (1); the 23 files with orientation 6 or 8 are stored landscape and shown portrait, the case the swap exists for. `sips` does not apply orientation, so the thumbnails first came out sideways; after rotating each by its database orientation (90 degrees for 6, 270 for 8) a contact sheet of all 24 was viewed: all upright and portrait (one is an inverted-color scan, still portrait).
