# Portrait pairs, milestone 3: renderer (2026-10-18)

Moved verbatim from `docs/DEVICE_TESTING.md` on 2026-10-19, with links updated for the new location.

Built and installed as before (187 JVM tests pass, 12 new: 8 for `PairColors` and the variant tables, 4 for the HEIF result-line parser; lint 0 errors, 26 warnings, unchanged). `SlideshowView` gained `showPair` (a second photo layer, a full-screen background layer, a caption inside each photo; every status and single-photo call first returns it to the old MATCH_PARENT layout), and `PairScene.build` draws the background off the main thread. A **debug-only** activity (`app/src/debug`, `PairPreviewActivity`, started with `adb shell am start -n com.googletv.screensaver/.PairPreviewActivity --ei index N`) shows the pairs listed in `files/pair-test.json` through the real decode, label, `PairFit`, and background code; the list was written with `adb` from a database query run in place on the Mac (read-only, `immutable=1`) and holds relative paths, so it stays off Git. The activity logs no path or label. The test list held 14 entries: all four styles with all three variants, 3:4 + 3:4, 3:4 + 2:3, stored-portrait (orientation 1) beside turned (orientation 6 and 8) photos, a pair on black, and a pair of 24 MP HEICs.

- **Screenshots (all 14, viewed by me):** every pair upright; both photos the same height; three equal slices (100/100/100 px for two 3:4 photos, 130/130/130 for 3:4 + 2:3, 99/99/101 and 91/91/93 for the odd widths of decoded sizes); labels inside their own photos; no black anywhere but the deliberate black pair. The four styles read as designed: tinted pinstripes and lattice in three pitches, wood grain with a dark rebate, a pale bevel and a soft shadow in walnut, oak, and cherry, and a linen mat with two thin lines in brass on charcoal, silver on navy, and brass on navy. Frame lines are clipped by the top and bottom of the screen as specified.
- **Timings:** background build 13 to 24 ms for pinstripes and brass, 40 to 66 ms for the lattice, 141 to 149 ms for the walnut frame. Decoding the two photos took 1.6 to 6.3 s for 12 MP photos (1.6 s was two small JPEGs) and 10.2 s for a 12 MP plus a 24 MP HEIC; **17.7 s for two 24 MP HEICs** (tile decodes of 8.3 and 5.7 s plus the SMB transfers), which is the milestone 4 timing risk (a 10 s Interval with a 17.7 s pair means the previous slide waits 7.7 s longer).
- **Finding 1 (fixed): 24 MP portraits were not portrait bitmaps.** The first pair of two 24 MP HEICs came out as two 1920 x 1080 bitmaps with the photo centered on black, because the native tile decoder always returns a screen-sized canvas. `PairFit` then shrank the pair to a fraction of the screen. The decoder's result line now carries `content=WxH+X+Y` and `NativeHeifDecoder` crops to it (810 x 1080 for a 4284 x 5712 photo). In Single mode the cropped bitmap is drawn at the same size and place as the canvas was, so the pixels are the same.
- **Finding 2 (fixed, pre-existing): part of some 24 MP photos was missing.** In the same pair one photo had a black strip along its right edge and another along its bottom (inside the picture, so invisible against the black letterbox in Single mode). The decoder log showed `grid=5x9 tileSize=896x640 offsets=700,0` for a 4284 x 5712 image: five columns of 896 minus 700 reach 3780 px, 504 px short of the image (12%), and the second photo had `offsets=0,592`, 544 px short of the bottom (9.5%). libheif's offset behaves like the tile size minus the crop (196 and 48 px). The decoder now uses the reported offset only when it covers the image and otherwise the tile size minus it; the re-run logged `used=196,0` and `used=0,48`, and both photos came out complete and matching a reference rendering of the same files (`sips`, rotated by the database orientation). A 4284 x 5712 photo with offsets 0,0 (`grid=5x6 tileSize=896x960`) is unaffected. **This changes Single mode too, but only by showing the part of such photos that was being dropped.** The rule is a heuristic from three photos; the native code has no unit test, and a wider check of 24 MP photos is still to do (milestone 4 decodes real pairs).
- **Observation, not changed:** a label on a very bright part of a photo is hard to read (a white caption with a 44% shadow on a sunlit wall). That is how single photos look today; the pair captions use the same style.
- **Restored:** the test list was only ever in the app's private files; the TV's settings and catalog were not touched (the app was only reinstalled). Screenshots and thumbnails in scratch (they include private pictures) were deleted.
- **Not covered:** the real player (milestone 4), a pair after a video or a status screen, memory with a preloaded pair, and the user's choice among the four styles.
