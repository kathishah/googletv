# Video playback in the in-app slideshow, milestone 4 (2026-10-17)

Moved verbatim from `docs/DEVICE_TESTING.md` on 2026-10-19, with links updated for the new location.

Built and installed as before (99 JVM tests pass, 21 of them new, covering `BufferSafeguard`, `VideoLabel`, `SlideMixer`, and the reader's buffered-ahead helpers; lint 0 errors). The preview was driven with `adb shell input keyevent`, and results read from `adb logcat -s PhotoSource VideoStage` (captured to a file, because the 1 MiB log ring overwrites early lines). The settings used were Media All with Type Videos, then Media Choose Albums with Type Photos + Videos, and the user's own settings were restored at the end (Media Choose Albums, Type Photos).

- **Orientation and proportions (user-checked):** portrait and landscape clips were upright and correctly proportioned, letterboxed, with no black frame between photos and videos.
- **20-clip soak** (Type Videos, Media All; 22 clips finished in about 15 minutes): 0 failures, **0 pauses**, start waits 0.2–1.1 s (median 0.66 s), every clip fully downloaded when it ended, a 198 s clip ended at 60.8 s (the cap), memory steady (PSS 90 MB over the first samples and 87 MB over the last, range 81–98 MB; Java heap 12–28 MB), and never more than one temp file in `cache/video-cache`, which holds the clip being played.
- **Heavy clips** (temporarily editing the app's private catalog copy to hold only the 71 Mbps 4K HLG clip and the 89 Mbps clip, restored afterwards): the 89 Mbps clip started after 6.8 s and finished with **one 0.76 s pause** inserted by the safeguard (`MediaPlayer.pause` and `start` exercised on the device); the 4K clip started after 4.2 s with no pauses; on a later pass the gate judged one start would take 61 s, skipped it for that pass ("retry later"), and later played it with no pauses. The user reported all clips played without hiccups.
- **Mixed sequence** (saved albums, Type Photos + Videos, 10 s interval): photos and videos alternated; about one video per 2 minutes, consistent with videos being about 8% of the pool. The shared SMB connection went idle during a 33 s clip and the source reconnected on its own ("Reused SMB connection failed; reconnecting"), as for any idle connection. The first slide took about 80 s in album mode because the existing code opens every selected photo to check it is readable; that is unchanged photo behavior.
- **Labels from video files:** of 9 clips, 7 had a date only, 1 had place and date, and 1 had none.
- **Finding and fix: missing videos.** About 18% of the catalog's videos were reported "file not found" and dropped at play time (35 of a 200-path sample, all under one long numeric folder). They are iCloud Shared Album copies (`ZSAVEDASSETTYPE` 4) whose files are not in `originals`. Catalog v3 excludes them: 2,667 library videos, album video memberships unchanged at 3,862, a 200-path sample with none missing, and a rebuild of 45.8 s.
- **Photos type unchanged:** with Type = Photos saved, the preview and the screensaver (started with `Somnambulator`) both read the album catalog as before and logged no video lines.
- **Not covered:** a soak of hours, whether HLG and Dolby Vision look right on this 100-nit panel (no problem was reported), and interaction with the TV's idle timers during a long preview.
