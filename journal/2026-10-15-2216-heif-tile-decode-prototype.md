# HEIF tile decode prototype (2026-10-15)

Moved verbatim from `docs/DEVICE_TESTING.md` on 2026-10-19, with links updated for the new location.

An isolated `heif-probe` diagnostic app uses pinned libheif 1.23.5 and libde265 1.1.3. From `apps/screensaver/`, `JAVA_HOME='/Applications/Android Studio.app/Contents/jbr/Contents/Home' ANDROID_HOME="$HOME/Library/Android/sdk" ./gradlew :heif-probe:assembleDebug` built the APK for the TV's `armeabi-v7a` ABI (the TV does not accept ARM64-only APKs). On 2026-10-15, the TV decoded all 45 HEVC tiles from a synthetic 5712×4284 grid in 2.5 seconds. A second synthetic grid with a 90° image transform decoded all 45 tiles in 2.4 seconds; screenshots showed the expected tile order and transformed orientation. The host harness also decoded both grids successfully.

Follow-up with real samples: a read-only Guest mount of the `Photos` share succeeded, although explicit Guest share listing still showed only `IPC$`; that listing alone did not predict direct share access. From a targeted first-25 HEIC/HEIF filesystem walk, a 5712×4284 sample was staged temporarily in the probe app's private files. On the TV, libheif reported the transformed image as 4284×5712, a 5×9 grid of 45 896×640 tiles, and assembled a 1920×1080 preview in 6,075 ms. The user visually confirmed that sample was correctly assembled and upright.

On 2026-10-15, a stratified set of 100 library HEICs at 24 MP or higher was decoded on the same TV: 47 at 5712×4284, 44 at 4284×5712, and 9 wide/panoramic images. **All 100 decoded successfully.** The decoder reported 6.885 s for the middle sample, a 10.457 s p95, and 13.303 s maximum; the slowest cases were wide images, including a 57.6 MP image (15300×3770) that decoded in 13.212 s. The selected samples were staged one at a time from the read-only mount; the script did not print photo paths. A six-image display preview (three landscape, three portrait) was visually reviewed by the user and confirmed correctly framed and upright. The staged source and temporary probe app were removed, and the production app was restored to the foreground. The 100-image run demonstrates broad decode coverage, but not startup latency, memory use under sustained slideshow playback, color fidelity, or complete transform coverage. Build and run instructions are in [the prototype README](../tools/heif_tile_prototype/README.md).
