# Portrait pairs, milestone 4: player integration (2026-10-18)

Moved verbatim from `docs/DEVICE_TESTING.md` on 2026-10-19, with links updated for the new location.

`SlideshowPlayer` takes an optional `PairingConfig`; with it, the shuffled slide list goes through `PairPlanner` (photos the source's `isPairable` reports, from the catalog's `portrait` list; videos and unknown photos stay single), a pair slide decodes both photos on the worker and builds its `PairScene`, and the previous slide stays up until all of it is ready. A pair that loses one photo is repaired by `PairFailureRules` (the other photo is shown alone; the failed one is dropped if the file can never load, otherwise sent to the back). `layout_mode` (`SINGLE` default) is stored; the Layout row is milestone 5, so these runs set it by editing the app's settings file with `adb`. Built as before (199 JVM tests pass, 12 new: `LayoutMode` 4, `PairFailureRules` 8; lint 0 errors, 26 warnings). The debug-only preview activity from milestone 3 was removed from the tree (it is in the git history of commit `6426378`). Log lines added under the `PhotoSource` tag (no names): `Layout Portrait pairs: N slides from M`, `Pair loaded: decode A + B ms, background C ms, style S`, `Slide shown: pair|single, T ms after the previous`.

- **Layout Single unchanged (TV check):** the user's own settings (11 albums, 10 s, no `layout_mode` stored) ran the preview for 3 minutes: 12 screenshots (viewed as a contact sheet) of ordinary single photos, portraits alone on black as before, and no `Layout`, `Slide shown`, or `Pair loaded` line at all (those are logged only when pairing is on), no crash. In code, `loadContent` returns `photoSource.loadImage(slide)` unwrapped when a slide has no partner, `showSlide` is the same call, and the new view layers stay hidden. Not byte-for-byte: the native HEIF decoder fix of milestone 3 now shows the missing part of some 24 MP photos in Single too; the same run's log shows it applied to 2 of 12 photos (`offsets=700,0 used=196,0`) and 1 more with `offsets=0,600 used=0,168`.
- **Real library, pairs on, 8 minutes (the user's 11 albums, 10 s):** `Layout Portrait pairs: 817 slides from 942 (125 pairs, 435 portrait photos)`, so 57% of the portraits paired (planner target 60%); 9 pairs and 44 singles shown, 9 screenshots of the pairs viewed: upright, equal height, three slices, the four styles, labels inside their photos, no blank frame. Longest pair 17.9 s after the previous slide (two 24 MP HEICs, decode 8.2 + 9.7 s).
- **Worst case, 24 MP only:** a temporary catalog of 40 randomly chosen 24 MP HEIC portraits (30 slides, 10 pairs, from the clone's database, in the TV's portrait list) ran 9 minutes: 15 pairs shown, **each took 14.7 to 21.9 s** (decode 7.0 to 13.1 s per photo, background 14 to 143 ms), the longest 21.9 s which is 2.2 times the 10 s Interval; the slides after a pair came on time (10.0 s). That is the figure to watch: at a 10 s Interval a pair of two 24 MP HEICs holds the previous slide 5 to 12 s longer, never blank; at 30 s or more it is invisible. No change was made; the options in the spec (a smaller second decode, a minimum pair interval) remain. PSS 70 to 134 MB over the run, native heap 10 to 39 MB.
- **Failure paths (temporary catalogs of readable portraits and nonexistent ones, so the share answers `STATUS_OBJECT_NAME_NOT_FOUND`):** with 20 readable and 40 missing photos, 19 of 41 slides were pairs; the log showed `One photo of a pair failed (dropped: PER_FILE ...), showing the other alone` 9 times in 4 minutes and `Skipping unreadable photo` for pairs with both photos missing, and the slideshow kept advancing every 10 s. A second catalog of 5 readable and 100 missing photos showed only the 5 real photos, one at a time, as the missing partners were dropped. The first listing attempt of both catalogs hit the start-up readability sample (`None of 8 sampled photos could be opened`), retried after 2 s and 4 s, and started. **Rule changed after this run:** my first version counted per-file partial failures toward the streak of three, which singles never did; it now counts only network-type failures. Not covered on the TV: a network-type failure of one photo of a pair and the stored artworks coming up for pairs (the Wi-Fi cannot be turned off while `adb` runs over the network; the code path for a whole failing pair is the existing single-photo one and the partial case is JVM-tested).
- **30-minute soak (pairs on, the user's 11 albums, 10 s, real catalog):** 31 minutes, 182 slides shown (149 single, 33 pairs of 811 slides, 131 pairs planned), the four styles 8, 9, 7, 9 times. PSS sampled every minute: **91 to 141 MB, first-half mean 114 MB, second-half mean 108 MB, trend -0.3 MB per minute** (Java heap 9 to 30 MB, native heap 12 to 44 MB), so no growth. Dwell: singles median 10.0 s, max 12.6 s; pairs median 10.0 s, max 17.1 s, 12 of 33 pairs over the Interval, none over 20 s. 243 screenshots (one every ~6 s, brightness only, deleted) showed no blank frame (none below 2% mean brightness). No crash, `AndroidRuntime`, failure or skipped-photo line.
- **Restored:** the saved settings and the real v5 catalog on the TV compared identical (`cmp`) to the copies taken first, the idle timeout is 600000, the DreamService is still selected, the temporary pair list was deleted from the TV, and the test catalogs, logs and screenshots in scratch were deleted at the end of the work.
