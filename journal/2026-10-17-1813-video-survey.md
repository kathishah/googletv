# Video survey (2026-10-17)

Moved verbatim from `docs/PHOTO_SOURCES.md` on 2026-10-19, with links updated for the new location. Inline corrections and updates are kept as originally written.

Input for the media-type feature ([roadmap item 9](../docs/ROADMAP.md)). Counts come from a copy of the clone's `Photos.sqlite` opened read-only and immutable, so they reflect the last checkpoint (2026-10-06) and omit about 11 days of newer assets. The query touched metadata only. Codec details come from `ffprobe` on a 30-file stratified sample (28 were still present); the sample over-represents rare formats, so its proportions are not the library's.

- **Count:** ~3,400 active videos (`ZKIND=1`, not trashed or hidden) next to ~35,000 active photos; 2 more videos are in Recently Deleted. 1,894 of the videos are in 378 regular albums.
- **Containers and size:** `.mov` 2,451 (~195 GB), `.mp4` 871 (~37 GB), `.m4v` 21 (1.5 GB), `.3gp` 6, `.avi` 5. About 230 GB in all, spread across 2011–2026.
- **Resolution (long edge):** 2,104 are 1080p-class (1920×1080 is the most common size), 907 are 720p-class, 336 are smaller, and 7 are 4K or larger.
- **Duration:** median 31.6 s, 90th percentile 110 s, 99th percentile 500 s, longest 2,994 s; 52.6 hours in total. 1,590 are under 30 s, 2,543 are 60 s or shorter, and 27 are over 10 minutes.
- **Flags:** 632 are marked HDR (type 5); in the sample these were HLG with Dolby Vision metadata. 653 carry subtype codes 100–103 (not mapped to Photos' names; the sample included 120 and 240 fps clips).
- **Shared-album copies:** 591 of the videos (18%) are iCloud Shared Album assets (`ZSAVEDASSETTYPE` 4) stored under `<id>/<album GUID>` folders that are not in `originals` on the clone; catalog v3 excludes them, leaving 2,667 usable videos.
- **Not usable as catalog videos:** 65 of the ~3,400 active videos are stored outside `originals` (an absolute directory) and 35 have no recorded file size, so catalog v2 listed 3,258 videos from a newer database state; v3 drops the shared-album copies above (see [media type](../docs/engineering/VIDEO_PLAYBACK.md#catalog)).
- **Sample formats:** H.264 14, HEVC 8 (4 Main 10 HLG with Dolby Vision), Motion JPEG 4, MPEG-4 Part 2 2. Audio: AAC 20 (one HE-AAC), PCM 4, AMR-NB 1, none 3. Frame rates from 14 to 240 fps; 6 clips carry rotation metadata (portrait). Video bitrate ranged from 0.43 to 70.8 Mbps with a median of 7.7 Mbps.
