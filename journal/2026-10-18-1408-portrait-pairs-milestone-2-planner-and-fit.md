# Portrait pairs, milestone 2: planner and fit (2026-10-18)

Moved verbatim from `docs/DEVICE_TESTING.md` on 2026-10-19, with links updated for the new location.

Pure Kotlin, so the exit check is the JVM suite; no TV step and nothing was installed. `cd apps/screensaver && JAVA_HOME='/Applications/Android Studio.app/Contents/jbr/Contents/Home' ANDROID_HOME="$HOME/Library/Android/sdk" ./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:assembleDebug :app:lintDebug`: 175 JVM tests pass (30 new: 20 for `PairPlanner`, 10 for `PairFit`), lint 0 errors, 26 warnings (unchanged).

- **`PairFit` reproduces the table:** two 3:4 photos 810 x 1080 each, 84% fill, slices 100 | 100 | 100 (photos at x = 100 and 1010); 3:4 + 2:3 810 and 720 px, 80%, slices of 130; two 2:3 720 px, 75%, slices of 160; two 9:16 about 608 px, 63%, slices of 233 to 236 (the spec's 235, give or take the rounding of 607.5 px). Over every aspect pair from 0.56 to 0.85 the height is the full 1080; the rectangles stay inside the screen and the slices add up to the free space, with the odd pixels on the right slice.
- **Spec corrected:** the first draft said the height is always the full 1080 for any pairable pair. Two photos at the 0.9 limit need 1944 px of photo at full height, so with the 16 px minimum slice they shrink to 1040 px (4%); two at 0.88 shrink to 1063 px. The formula in the spec is now `H = min(screen height, (screen width - 3 x 16) / (aspect1 + aspect2))`; the 16 px "gutter" is the minimum slice. Pairs this wide are rare (17 photos between 0.78 and 0.9).
- **Planner:** with probability 1 every pairable photo pairs except an odd one out; with 0 nothing pairs; a fixed seed gives a fixed plan; videos, landscapes and unknown photos stay single in place; every photo appears exactly once (as a slide or a partner) over 20 seeded shuffles; the coin is flipped only for pairable photos, so inserting landscapes does not change which portraits pair; consecutive pairs never share a style in 200 seeded plans, including from the last pair to the first (the player loops the list); every style and variant appears; one allowed style repeats and no allowed style gives pairs on black.
- **Simulation:** 10,000 photos, 33% portrait: 3,322 portraits, 1,033 pairs, 62.2% of portraits paired in that one run; over 300 runs of 2,000 photos the mean was **59.9%**, so the 0.6 probability comes out unbiased. A pool of 100 photos averaged 90.4 slides and 9.6 pairs (11.5% of slides, as the spec predicts); the four styles were within 248 to 264 of 1,033.
- **Not covered:** anything on the TV or on screen; the renderer is milestone 3.
