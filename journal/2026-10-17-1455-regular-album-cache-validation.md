# Regular-album cache validation (2026-10-17)

Moved verbatim from `docs/PHOTO_SOURCES.md` on 2026-10-19, with links updated for the new location. Inline corrections and updates are kept as originally written.

The existing `tools/generate_photos_album_index.py` is research/validation tooling, not the runtime architecture. It confirmed the observed query over a local read-only clone: regular albums are `ZGENERICALBUM` entity 34 with `ZKIND=2`, joined through `Z_34ASSETS` to `ZASSET`; `ZUUID` is used as an opaque album ID. The Android implementation runs this query against a staged local read-only SQLite database and writes a versioned app-private JSON cache. It rejects unsupported extensions and unsafe relative paths, and startup opens a sample of up to eight photos to check readability. The cached catalog and persistent cover thumbnails have been displayed on the TCL. TV refresh timings are recorded in [album selection](2026-10-17-1617-album-refresh-timings.md) and [device testing](../docs/DEVICE_TESTING.md#device-findings): the first full sync took 900.7 s, an unchanged cached refresh later took 4.5 s with no bytes copied, and an incremental WAL refresh after CCC growth took 50.6 s. Sequential DB/WAL copying does not prove a coherent snapshot if the library changes during transfer; live-refresh consistency remains unverified.

Broader spot validation on 2026-10-17 checked one asset reference from each of 24 distinct regular albums: 22 were existing, readable supported image files under `originals`; two were excluded as unsupported or video; none were missing/unreadable or out of root. A full generator pass then checked the clone: ~760 regular album candidates, 756 with links, 38,431 membership links, and 17,474 distinct database assets; all ~760 candidates had distinct, non-null `ZUUID` values. Of membership rows after within-album duplicate collapse, 33,302 mapped to readable supported files under `originals`, 4,010 were unsupported or video, 3 were missing, 1,116 pointed outside `originals`, and 0 were unreadable. There were no duplicate paths within an album. The index contains 33,302 album-path references to 15,091 unique usable paths; 11,041 paths are shared by multiple regular albums and the TV deduplicates them. These results apply to this clone only. After an actual CCC refresh on 2026-10-17, the saved selection still worked, providing one check of UUID continuity; a title edit and longer-term continuity remain unverified.

The former design generated a 1.6 MB sample JSON sidecar at `/tmp/PhotosAlbumIndex.json`; it was not written to the mounted share because that mount is read-only. On 2026-10-17, the mounted clone's `Photos.sqlite` measured 1,280,086,016 bytes and its WAL measured 6,370,653,032 bytes (7,650,739,048 bytes total, 7.12 GiB). The TCL app-data filesystem reported 44 GiB available. The Android copy UI shows combined byte progress. The first full transfer took 900.7 s; after staged synchronization was added, unchanged refreshes took 3–4.5 s and one incremental post-CCC refresh took 50.6 s. See [measured refresh timings](2026-10-17-1617-album-refresh-timings.md). The live clone's consistency during a TV refresh and future UUID continuity remain open.
